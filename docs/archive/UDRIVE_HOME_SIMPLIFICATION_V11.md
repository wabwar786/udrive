# UDrive Home Simplification V11

- Repaired the broken service-card composition by replacing screenshot-like card crops with isolated transparent vehicle photo assets.
- Preserved the dark/lime service-card layout without nested duplicate cards or image overflow.
- Home now shows only the latest two customer bookings, ordered by actual booking creation time.
- Tapping a recent booking resolves its saved destination and opens the vehicle/rate selection screen directly; the destination is not requested again.
- The direct booking screen uses the booking type to choose City or Tours flow.
- Vehicle selection continues to show per-seat and whole-vehicle prices from the database/catalog API. Matching tour inventory displays available seats and the publishing driver.
- Standard Where to flow remains available for a new destination.
