UDrive is the local ride and travel app for Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Book a car,
bike or coster in minutes, agree the fare before the trip starts, and travel
with drivers whose documents have been checked.

BOOK A RIDE
• City rides and multi-day tours in one app
• See the recommended fare first, or offer your own price
• Compare offers from nearby drivers and accept the one you like
• Pay the driver directly — no card needed

TRAVEL SAFELY
• Every driver and vehicle is verified with CNIC, licence and registration papers
• A trip OTP makes sure you get into the right vehicle
• Live trip tracking, in-app chat and a direct call button
• SOS button and trusted contacts for emergencies

EXPLORE KASHMIR
• Tours to Neelum, Sharda, Rawalakot, Banjosa Lake and Pir Chinasi
• Whole-vehicle or per-seat booking for groups and families
• Hotel bookings with local partners
• Route maps and fare estimates before you book

DRIVE WITH UDRIVE
• Register your vehicle, upload your documents and get approved
• Receive ride requests near you and send your own fare offer
• Track earnings, commission and wallet balance in the app

UDrive is built in Azad Kashmir, for Azad Kashmir. The app is available in
English, with Urdu across the menus and account screens.

NOTES — claims removed from the full description, and why
================================================================
Keep these out unless the app changes. Each was checked in code.

1. "rickshaw"  — removed.
   The bookable catalogue is Car, Bike, Coster and Hiace. Rickshaws are
   explicitly excluded from the Home flow (core/vehicles/nearby_vehicle.dart).
   A reviewer looking for a rickshaw finds no way to book one.

2. "Arang Kel" and "Leepa" — removed.
   Leepa appears in no reachable tour surface at all. Arang Kel is a tour chip
   but is not in the server destination catalogue that Explore renders
   (migration 003 seeds Muzaffarabad, Neelum Valley, Sharda, Rawalakot,
   Banjosa Lake, Pir Chinasi). The list now names only those.

3. "city-to-city trips" as a separate product — removed.
   The City-to-city tile runs the identical city flow and every request is
   posted with serviceType 'City'. There is no distinct intercity product.

4. "Available in English and Urdu" — softened.
   The language toggle is real and Urdu covers the menus, settings and account
   screens, but Home, vehicle selection, live tracking, SOS and hotels are
   hard-coded English. Restore the original wording once those are localised.

5. "coaster" spelling — the app and the server both say "Coster". The listing
   now matches, so a search for the word in the app finds it.
