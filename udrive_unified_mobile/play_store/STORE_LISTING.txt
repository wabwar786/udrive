FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
----------------------------------------------------------------
UDrive is the local ride and travel app for Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Book a car,
motorcycle, rickshaw or coaster in minutes, agree the fare before the trip
starts, and travel with drivers whose documents have been checked.

BOOK A RIDE
• City rides, city-to-city trips and multi-day tours in one app
• See the recommended fare first, or offer your own price
• Compare offers from nearby drivers and accept the one you like
• Pay the driver directly — no card needed

TRAVEL SAFELY
• Every driver and vehicle is verified with CNIC, licence and registration papers
• A trip OTP makes sure you get into the right vehicle
• Live trip tracking, in-app chat and a direct call button
• SOS button and trusted contacts for emergencies

EXPLORE KASHMIR
• Tours to Neelum, Arang Kel, Leepa, Rawalakot, Banjosa and Pir Chinasi
• Whole-vehicle or per-seat booking for groups and families
• Hotel bookings with local partners
• Offline maps for valleys with weak signal

DRIVE WITH UDRIVE
• Register your vehicle, upload your documents and get approved
• Receive ride requests near you and send your own fare offer
• Track earnings, commission and wallet balance in the app

UDrive is built in Azad Kashmir, for Azad Kashmir. Available in English and Urdu.
