# Complete Driver Verification Features

- Click any Driver card to open a full-height review panel.
- Full Driver registration information.
- CNIC front and back previews.
- Driving licence preview.
- Driver selfie preview.
- Protected authenticated image/PDF loading.
- Open and download controls.
- Missing-document indicators.
- Driver languages and service areas.
- Emergency contact and payout details.
- All vehicles shown inside the same Driver review.
- Vehicle front, rear and interior photographs.
- Registration book, insurance and fitness certificate.
- Verify/request changes/suspend each vehicle without leaving the Driver.
- Approve/request changes/reject Driver from the same panel.
- Review notes required for negative decisions.
- Confirmation before destructive decisions.
- VerificationOfficer role access to review APIs and protected files.

Driver approval remains server-protected: all four required Driver
documents and at least one verified vehicle are required.
