## 1. What UDrive is, and what it is not

UDrive is a **marketplace**. We connect people who want a ride, a tour or a hotel
room with independent drivers and businesses who provide them.

**We are not a transport company.** We do not own the vehicles, we do not employ
the drivers, and we do not carry you ourselves. The contract for the journey is
between you and the driver. Our job is to introduce you, to give you the tools to
agree a fare, to verify that the driver and the vehicle are real, and to keep a
record of what happened.

This matters for what we are and are not answerable for — see section 13.

## 4. How a fare is agreed

UDrive prices a trip on the server and shows you a band: a minimum, a
recommendation and a maximum.

- You offer a fare. You may offer the recommendation, or more, or less — but not
  below the minimum and not above the maximum. Whenever your app has the
  server's quote for that trip, those limits are enforced by us and cannot be
  worked around.
- Drivers nearby see your request and either accept your fare or answer with
  their own, which is held to the same limits.
- You choose an offer. That creates the booking and assigns that driver.
- The band is built from the distance, the time, the vehicle, the fuel price and
  the terrain of the route. It is not a bid for the lowest possible price: the
  floor exists so that a trip a driver cannot afford to make is never offered.

**Fixed per-seat routes.** Some Coster routes are sold at a published fare per
seat. On those there is no bidding at all, by either side.

**Tours** are priced by each driver on their own vehicle, not by us.

The fare is what you and the driver agreed. Waiting time, extra stops, tolls or a
changed destination are between you and the driver, and should be settled before
they happen.

## 5. Paying

**Rides are settled directly between you and the driver**, normally in cash at
the end of the trip. UDrive does not take your money for a ride and does not
process card payments.

**Tours** require an advance of at least **20% of the total** to confirm a
booking. You may pay more. A confirmed tour booking may be cancelled free of
charge within **5 minutes** of the advance being paid; after that the tour
operator's own cancellation terms apply.

**Drivers pay us a commission**, currently **10%** of the fare, taken from the
driver's commission balance when the trip starts. Drivers keep that balance
topped up in the app. If the balance runs out, a driver simply stops being sent
new requests — nothing is deducted from anybody's fare and trips already running
are unaffected. We will tell drivers in the app before we change the commission
rate.

**Refunds**, where they apply, are reviewed by our finance team against the
booking record. A cancellation fee may be deducted where the cancellation caused
a driver a real loss.

## 6. Cancelling

Cancelling a request before anyone has accepted costs nothing and needs no
reason.

Cancelling a **booking** affects a driver who has committed and may already be
driving to you. Do it as early as you can, and tell us why. Repeated late
cancellations may cost you access to UDrive.

A driver who repeatedly accepts and then cancels may be suspended.
