chahiye, un azad drivers aur karobaron se milate hain jo yeh dete hain.

**Hum transport company nahi hain.** Gaariyan hamari nahi, drivers hamare
mulazim nahi, aur hum khud aap ko kahin nahi le jate. Safar ka muahida aap ke
aur driver ke darmiyan hota hai. Hamara kaam yeh hai ke aap ka taaruf karayein,
fare tay karne ke auzaar dein, tasdeeq karein ke driver aur gaari asli hain, aur
jo hua us ka record rakhein.

Yeh baat is liye ahem hai ke hum kis cheez ke zimmedar hain aur kis ke nahi —
dekhein hissa 13.

## 4. Fare kaise tay hota hai

UDrive safar ki qeemat server par banata hai aur aap ko ek hadd dikhata hai: ek
kam se kam, ek tajweez, aur ek zyada se zyada.

- Aap fare pesh karte hain. Tajweez ke barabar, us se zyada, ya us se kam — magar
  kam se kam wali hadd se neeche nahi aur zyada wali hadd se upar nahi. Jab bhee

  hai. Yeh sab se sasti boli ka muqabla nahi hai: neeche wali hadd is liye hai

**Fixed per-seat route.** Kuch Coster route har seat ke ek mutayyan fare par
bikte hain. Un par koi bid nahi hoti — na aap ki, na driver ki.

**Tour** ki qeemat har driver apni gaari par khud rakhta hai, hum nahi.

Fare wohi hai jo aap aur driver ne tay kiya. Intezar ka waqt, raaste mein rukna,
toll, ya destination badalna — yeh aap aur driver ke darmiyan hai aur hone se
pehle tay kar lena chahiye.

## 5. Adaigi

**Ride ka hisab seedha aap aur driver ke darmiyan hota hai**, aam tor par safar
ke aakhir mein naqad. UDrive ride ka paisa apne paas nahi leta aur card ki
adaigi nahi chalata.

**Tour** ke liye booking pakki karne ke waste kul raqam ka **kam az kam 20%**
pesh-gi dena hota hai. Aap is se zyada bhee de sakte hain. Pakki hui tour
booking pesh-gi ada karne ke **5 minute** ke andr bila kisi kharche ke cancel ho
sakti hai; us ke baad tour chalane wale ki apni shartein lagti hain.

**Drivers hamein commission dete hain** — is waqt fare ka **10%**, jo trip shuru
hote hi driver ke commission balance se kat jata hai. Driver yeh balance app
mein khud bharte hain. Balance khatam ho jaye to driver ko nayi requests milna
band ho jati hain — kisi ke fare se kuch nahi kata jata aur chalti hui trips par
koi asar nahi parta. Commission ki sharah badalne se pehle hum drivers ko app
mein bata dein ge.

**Refund**, jahan banta ho, hamari finance team booking ke record se milakar
dekhti hai. Jahan cancel karne se driver ka asal nuqsan hua ho, wahan
cancellation fee kaati ja sakti hai.
