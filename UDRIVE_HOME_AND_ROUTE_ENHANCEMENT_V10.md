# UDrive Home and Route Enhancement V10

Implemented:
- Photographic vehicle artwork on service blocks, vehicle lists, ride cards, and splash screen.
- Restored bright lime background shapes on all four home service blocks.
- Route popup now uses persisted recent destination searches instead of fixed D-17/F-10/Blue Area suggestions.
- Added City-to-city / Tour booking selector.
- Added Seat booking / Full vehicle selector before destination selection.
- From and To remain editable with live location search.
- Route popup resizes with keyboard and its result list remains scrollable.
- Added Back and Home actions to route and vehicle screens.
- Explore Kashmir now opens in a Scaffold with Back and Home actions.
- Removed the large route-summary black overlay from the vehicle map screen.
- Vehicle selection cards now use photographic vehicle images rather than Material clipart icons.

New assets are under assets/vehicles_photo and registered in pubspec.yaml.
