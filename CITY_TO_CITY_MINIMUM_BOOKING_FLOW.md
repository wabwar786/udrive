# City-to-City minimum booking flow

Updated only the City-to-City customer flow.

## Customer flow
1. Open City-to-City Ride.
2. Pickup is selected/current location and customer chooses destination.
3. Choose a ride card and tap either `1 Seat` or `Full` with the final estimated fare shown directly on the card.
4. A compact confirmation sheet shows route, vehicle, seats (only when per-seat), total fare and Cash payment. Tap `Confirm Ride`.
5. The app creates the ride request and shows `Finding your driver...`.
6. The best verified available offer is selected automatically based on safety score, rating, ETA, then fare.
7. Booking confirmation shows driver, vehicle, ETA, total and live tracking action.

## Removed from the City-to-City customer UI
- Separate fare/per-km information boxes.
- Editable customer fare field.
- Separate global Per Seat / Whole Vehicle mode section.
- Separate Book Selected Ride footer step.
- Manual driver-offer comparison/selection for City-to-City.

Tours & Trips and Private Vehicle keep their existing flows for now.
