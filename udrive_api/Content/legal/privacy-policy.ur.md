## Mukhtasar baat

Hum aap ka phone number aur naam lete hain taake aap ka account ban sake, aap ka
pickup aur destination lete hain taake ride ho sake, aur agar aap UDrive par
gaari chalate hain to aap ka CNIC, driving licence aur gaari ki registration
lete hain — sirf yeh tasdeeq karne ke liye ke aap wahi hain jo aap keh rahe hain
aur gaari asli hai.

**Aap ke shanakhti kaghazat sirf tasdeeq ke liye istemal hote hain, aur kisi
kaam ke liye nahi.** Hum inhein bechte nahi. Hum aap ka data kisi
ishtiharati company, data bechne walon, ya kisi aise bande ko nahi dete jo usay
apne faide ke liye chahta ho.

UDrive mein **koi analytics nahi, koi crash-reporting nahi, koi ishtihar nahi,
aur kisi qism ka koi tracking software nahi** hai. Hum aap ki advertising ID,
IMEI, MAC address ya kisi bhee hardware fingerprint ko parhte hi nahi. App ke
andr koi cheez aisi nahi jo aap ki harkat dekh kar kahin bhej rahi ho. Yeh soch
samajh kar kiya gaya faisla hai, ittefaq nahi, aur aap hum se is ka hisab maang
sakte hain.

## 5. Location — theek theek kab

**Hum kabhi bhee background mein aap ki location nahi lete.** App background
location ki ijazat maangti hi nahi aur na koi background service chalati hai.
App band hoti hai to kuch nahi liya jata. Aap khud dekh sakte hain: Android app
location ke liye sirf `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` aur `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`
maangti hai. Na `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` hai, na foreground-service ki
ijazat.

**Customer.** App khuli ho to hum aap ki jagah parhte hain taake map wahan
markooz ho, aas paas ki gaariyan dikhein, aap ke rakhe hue pin ka pata ban sake,
aur pickup khud bhar jaye. Yeh istemal ho kar khatam ho jati hai — aap kahan
kahan gaye, is ka koi record hum nahi rakhte. Aap ki jagah sirf tab mehfooz hoti
hai jab woh aap ke apne banaye hue record ka hissa ban jaye: booking ka pickup
aur destination, koi safety report, ya emergency alert.

**Driver, jab online aur khali hon.** Taqreeban har 15 second baad hum aap ki
jagah likhte hain taake aas paas ka customer dekh sake ke ek gaari mojood hai.
Hum **har driver ki ek hi row rakhte hain aur usay upar likh dete hain** — aap
kahan kahan khare rahe, is ka koi record nahi banta.

**Driver, trip ke dauran.** Har chand second baad — theek waqfa hum tay karte
hain aur woh 1 se 60 second ke darmiyan hota hai — hum aap ki jagah, uski
durustagi, rukh, raftar, phone ki battery, aur yeh ke location ki ijazat mili
hui hai ya nahi, likhte hain, taake customer aur hamara operations desk dekh
sake ke safar chal raha hai. **Yeh record 30 din
baad mita diya jata hai.**

Trip ke dauran location lena tab shuru hota hai jab aap trip qubool karte hain
aur trip khatam hote hi ruk jata hai. Signal chala jaye to yeh aap ke phone
mein ruk jati hain aur signal aate hi chali jati hain.

kar sake aur baad mein record mojood ho.

Feature ka maqsad hi yehi hai, magar dabane se pehle aap ko theek theek pata
hona chahiye ke kya nikalta hai aur kis ke paas jata hai.

**App koi awaz record nahi karti. Microphone istemal nahi hota.**

## 8. Aur kaun aap ki maloomat dekhta hai

Hum chand service providers istemal karte hain. Woh hamari hidayat par, neeche
likhe maqasid ke liye data par kaam karte hain — aur kisi kaam ke liye nahi.

**Google** — map aur pate ke liye.

- Jab aap pata dhoondte hain, aap ka likha hua matn aur aap ki taqreebi jagah
  Google Places ko jati hai.
- Jab aap map par pin rakhte hain, woh coordinates Google ke geocoder ko jate
  hain taake un ka pata ban sake.
- Jab raasta kheencha jata hai, shuru aur aakhir ke coordinates Google ki routing
  service ko jate hain.
- Map ke tiles Google ki tile service se aate hain, jis se yeh zahir hota hai ke
  aap map ka kaunsa hissa dekh rahe hain.
- Map khud Google ke Maps SDK se banta hai jo **app ke andr** hai aur aap ke
  phone se **seedha** Google se baat karta hai. Jab neela nuqta dikh raha ho, to
  Google aap ki jagah dekh raha hota hai. Yeh har us app ke sath hota hai jo
  Google ka map istemal karti hai.

**WA Engine** — hamara apna WhatsApp relay, jo hum Railway par chalate hain.
Usay wasool karne wale ka number aur paighaam ka matn milta hai. Login code ki
soorat mein iska matlab code khud hai. SOS ki soorat mein aap ka naam aur aap ke
coordinates.

**OpenStreetMap** — jab Google mojood na ho to pate ki talash aur map ke liye.
Usay talash ka matn ya coordinates, aur aap ka IP address milta hai.

**Google Fonts** — app pehli baar chalne par apna font download karti hai, is
liye us lamhe Google aap ka IP address dekhta hai.

**Apple** — iPhone par ek screen (tour ka pickup chunna) coordinates ka pata
banane ke liye phone ka apna geocoder istemal karti hai, jo Apple ka hai.
Android par wohi screen Google ka istemal karti hai.

**Unsplash** — jab hamari hotel listings load na ho sakein to app namoone ki
listings dikhati hai jin ki tasweerein Unsplash par hain. Woh tasweerein khulne
par Unsplash aap ka IP address dekh leta hai. Aap ya aap ke safar ke baare mein
kuch nahi jata.

**Railway** — hamare servers, database aur aap ki bheji hui files yahan rehti
hain.

Bas, yehi poori fehrist hai. Is ke alawa koi nahi.

## 13. Security

- Aap ke phone aur hamare server ke darmiyan har cheez HTTPS par chalti hai. App
  saada HTTP qubool hi nahi karti.
- Staff accounts ke passwords PBKDF2 se badal kar rakhe jate hain, kabhi saada
  matn mein nahi.
- Login ke code ek-tarfa hash ki shakal mein rakhe jate hain, is liye koi
  bhee — hum samet — database se code parh nahi sakta.
- Bheje hue kaghazat bay-tarteeb naam ke sath rakhe jate hain. Woh us bande ko
  diye jate hain jis ke woh hain, un staff ko jinka kaam tasdeeq hai, aur tanaze
  ki soorat mein us case ko dekhne wale staff ko. Do cheezein jaan boojh kar is
  se zyada dikhti hain: driver ki tasweer aur gaari ki tasweer us safar ke
  customer ko dikhai jati hai, taake aap ko pata ho kaun lene aa raha hai.
- Kisi ke record par staff jo bhee karta hai, woh kis ne aur kab kiya — sab
  likha jata hai.

Hadd bhee saaf bata dein: aap ke kaghazat ek aam encrypted server disk par
rehte hain, alag chaabiyon wale mehfooz sandooq mein nahi. Koi nizaam sau fiisad
mehfooz nahi hota. Aisa kuch ho jo aap par asar dale, to hum aap ko batayenge.
