**UDrive** is operated by **Tech Geni Ltd.** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
we collect when you use the UDrive app, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it,
and what you can ask us to do about it.

This English version governs. The Roman Urdu version is provided for convenience;
if the two ever disagree, this one applies.

## The short version

We collect your phone number and name so you can have an account, your pickup and
destination so a ride can happen, and — if you drive for UDrive — your CNIC,
driving licence and vehicle registration so we can check you are who you say you
are and that the vehicle is real.

**We use your identity documents for verification and nothing else.** We do not
sell them. We do not rent, trade or share your data with advertisers, data
brokers or anyone who wants it for their own purposes.

UDrive contains **no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no
tracking software of any kind**. We do not read your advertising ID, your IMEI,
your MAC address or any hardware fingerprint. There is nothing in the app
watching what you do and reporting it somewhere else. This is a deliberate
choice, not an accident, and you can hold us to it.

## 2. What we collect from everyone

**Your account**

- Your mobile number. This is how we identify your account.
- Your name, as you enter it.
- The language you choose.

**Signing in**

- A 4-digit code we send to your number on WhatsApp. We store only a
  one-way scrambled form of the code, never the code itself.
- The IP address the request came from.
- Your app or browser's user-agent string.
- A device identifier **the app generates for itself** — a number based on the
  moment you first opened the app, stored on your phone. It is not your
  advertising ID, your IMEI, or any identifier that follows you between apps. It
  resets if you reinstall.

## 3. What we collect from customers

- Where you are going: pickup and destination, as both a place name and
  coordinates.
- Trip details: date and time, seats, number of adults and children, luggage,
  and any note you write for the driver.
- Your preferences for a trip, if you set them — for example women-only or
  family-only travel.
- The fare you offer, the offers drivers make, which one you accept, and the
  full history of what happened during the booking.
- Messages you send the driver in the trip chat.
- Ratings and any review text you write.
- Complaints, disputes, the files you attach as evidence, and support tickets.
- Trusted contacts you add for safety: their name, phone number and relationship
  to you.
- An emergency contact name and number, if you add one to a booking.
- For tours: passenger names, gender and age group, and a partly hidden phone
  number for each passenger.
- Hotel bookings: dates, number of guests and rooms.
- If you register a business or hotel: its name, address, phone number and
  position on the map.

## 5. Location — exactly when

**We never collect your location in the background.** The app does not ask for
background-location permission and does not run a background service. When the
app is closed, nothing is collected. You can check this yourself: the only
location permissions the Android app declares are `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and
`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`. There is no `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` and no
foreground-service permission.

**Customers.** While the app is open, we read your position to centre the map,
show vehicles near you, turn a pin you drop into an address, and fill in your
pickup. This is used and discarded — we do not keep a history of where you have
been. Your position is saved only when it becomes part of a record you created:
the pickup and destination on a booking, a safety report, or an emergency alert.

**Drivers, while online and waiting.** About every 15 seconds we record where you
are, so a nearby customer can see that a vehicle is available. We keep **one row
per driver and overwrite it** — there is no history of your waiting positions.

**Drivers, during a trip.** Every couple of seconds — the exact interval is set
by us and is between 1 and 60 seconds — we record your position, accuracy,
heading, speed, your phone's battery level and whether location permission is
granted, so the customer and our operations desk can see the trip is
progressing. **This trail is deleted after 30 days.**

Location collection during a trip starts when you accept and stops when the trip
ends. If you lose signal, pings are held on your phone and sent when you are back
online.

## 6. Safety and emergencies

If you raise an SOS in the app, we send a WhatsApp message to the trusted
contacts you have added and to our safety number — up to twelve numbers in total.
That message contains **your name, your exact coordinates, a Google Maps link to
that position, the accuracy of the reading and the time**. We also record an
emergency case with those coordinates so our team can act on it and so there is a
record afterwards.

This is the whole point of the feature, but you should know precisely what goes
out and to whom before you press it.

The app does not record audio. The microphone is not used.

more. Drivers also carry a safety score, which is shown to customers and helps
order who is offered a trip; today it is the same starting value for everyone
and nothing in the platform changes it. If that ever stops being true we will
say here what moves it.

## 8. Who else receives your information

We use a small number of service providers. They process data on our
instructions, for the purposes below, and for nothing else.

**Google** — for maps and addresses.

- When you search for an address, your search text and your approximate position
  go to Google Places.
- When you drop a pin, those coordinates go to Google's geocoder to turn them
  into an address.
- When a route is drawn, the start and end coordinates go to Google's routing
  service.
- The map tiles themselves are fetched from Google's tile service, which implies
  the part of the map you are looking at.
- The map is drawn by Google's Maps SDK **inside the app**, which talks to
  Google directly from your phone. When the blue dot is showing, Google sees your
  position. This is true of every app that uses a Google map.

**WA Engine** — our own WhatsApp relay, which we run on Railway. It receives the
recipient's number and the message body. For a login code, that means the code
itself. For an SOS, that means your name and your coordinates.

**OpenStreetMap** — used for address search and map tiles when Google is not
available. It receives the search text or coordinates, and your IP address.

**Google Fonts** — the app downloads its typeface the first time it runs, so
Google sees your IP address at that moment.

**Apple** — on an iPhone, one screen (picking up for a tour) turns coordinates
into an address using the phone's own geocoder, which is Apple's. On Android the
same screen uses Google's.

**Railway** — hosts our servers, our database and the files you upload.

That is the complete list. There are no others.

## 9. What we never do

- We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.
- We never give your identity documents to anyone for marketing, credit scoring,
  background-check resale or any other purpose.
- We do not run advertising in UDrive and we do not share anything with
  advertising networks.
- There is no analytics tool, no crash-reporting tool and no attribution tool in
  the app, the website or the admin system.
- We do not use your data to train any product other than UDrive itself.

The only circumstance in which we would hand your information to someone else is
a lawful order from a court or a competent authority in Azad Jammu & Kashmir or
Pakistan, or where it is necessary to protect someone's life. If that happens and
we are permitted to tell you, we will.

## 10. How long we keep things

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Login codes | Deleted 24 hours after they expire |
| Sign-in sessions, with their IP address and user-agent | 30 days after the session expires or you sign out |
| A driver's location trail during trips | **30 days** |
| Public trip-tracking links you share | 7 days |
| Notifications you have read | 180 days |
| Verification documents and photographs | While you drive for UDrive. Deleted when you delete your account |
| Bookings, fares, payments, ratings, disputes | While your account exists. After you delete your account they remain **without your name**, because the other party to the trip, our accounts and the law all need the record to survive |
| Our internal log of what staff did | Kept as an accountability record |

We are not going to promise you a tidy number of years for the records in the
last two rows, because we would have to keep them for as long as a dispute or a
tax question about that trip could still arise, and we would rather tell you that
plainly than print a figure we do not honour.

## 12. Deleting your account

In the app: **Settings → Delete account**, then type DELETE to confirm. You can
also ask us on WhatsApp.

You cannot delete your account in the middle of a live trip. Finish or cancel it
first.

**What is erased**

- Your email address is deleted outright. Your name and phone number are
  replaced with placeholders, and your number is freed immediately, so you or
  anyone else can register it again.
- Every session everywhere is ended.
- Your trusted contacts are deleted outright.
- Any emergency notes on your profile.
- For drivers: CNIC number, licence number, date of birth, residential address,
  emergency contact and bank details are erased, your saved payout account is
  deleted, **and your uploaded CNIC, licence, selfie, registration book, vehicle
  photographs and wallet top-up screenshots are deleted from our storage.**
- Your last known position is deleted.
- Your vehicles are taken out of service and their registration numbers are
  freed for use again.

**What remains, and why**

These survive, but without your name on them:

- bookings, fares and payments;
- ratings and reviews, and any dispute you were part of, including files
  attached to it as evidence — that evidence belongs to a case with another
  party to it, and removing one side's would rewrite a dispute somebody else
  still relies on;
- messages you sent the driver or the customer in a trip chat;
- any emergency alert or safety report you raised, including where it was
  raised from;
- our internal log of what our own staff did.

A driver's location trail from trips is not deleted immediately; it ages out on
its usual 30-day schedule.

We keep these because the other person on that trip has a right to their own
record of it, our accounts must balance, and a dispute or a tax question can
arrive months later. What is left is a trip that happened, not a person.

## 13. Security

- Everything between your phone and our servers travels over HTTPS. The app
  refuses plain HTTP outright.
- Passwords for staff accounts are stored scrambled with PBKDF2, never as text.
- Login codes are stored as a one-way hash, so nobody — including us — can read
  a code from the database.
- Uploaded documents are stored under random file names. They are served to the
  person they belong to, to the staff who do verification, and — for a dispute —
  to the staff handling that case. Two of them are shown more widely on purpose:
  a driver's photograph and the vehicle's photograph are shown to the customer
  on that trip, so you know who is picking you up.
- Every action a staff member takes on someone's record is logged with who did
  it and when.

We should be straight with you about the limits: your documents are stored on an
ordinary encrypted server disk, not in an encrypted vault with separate keys. No
system is perfectly safe. If something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell
you.

## 14. Children

UDrive is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from
a child. Children travel as passengers on a booking made by an adult, and in that
case we hold only what the adult tells us — the number of children travelling,
and for a tour, a name and age group.

If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.

If we change something that matters, we will raise the version number and the

effect. The current version is always readable in the app, without an internet
connection, at **Settings → Privacy Policy**.
