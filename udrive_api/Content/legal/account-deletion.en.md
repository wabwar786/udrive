You can delete your UDrive account yourself, from inside the app. You do not have
to ask us and you do not have to give a reason.

## In the app

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Type **DELETE** to confirm.

That is all. It takes effect immediately.

You cannot delete your account in the middle of a live trip — finish it or cancel
it first, then try again.

## If you cannot open the app

Message us on WhatsApp: **0335-6823975**, from the number the account is
registered to. We will verify the number and delete the account for you.

## What is erased

- Your email address is deleted outright. Your name and phone number are
  replaced with placeholders. **Your mobile number is released immediately**, so
  it can be registered again — by you or by anybody else.
- Every signed-in session, on every device, ends at once.
- Your trusted contacts are deleted outright.
- If you drove for UDrive: your CNIC number, driving licence number, date of
  birth, residential address, emergency contact and bank details are erased,
  your saved payout account is deleted, and **your uploaded CNIC, licence,
  selfie, registration book, vehicle photographs and wallet top-up screenshots
  are deleted from our storage.**
- Your last known position is deleted.
- Your vehicles are removed from service and their registration numbers are
  freed.

## What remains, and why

Records of trips that happened survive, but **without your name attached**:
bookings, fares and payments; ratings and reviews; any dispute you were part of,
including files attached to it as evidence; the messages you sent in a trip
chat; and any emergency alert or safety report you raised. A driver's location
trail from trips ages out on its usual 30-day schedule rather than going at
once.

We keep them for three reasons: the other person on that trip has a right to
their own record of it, our accounts have to balance, and a dispute or a tax
question about a trip can arrive months after it happened. What is left behind is
a journey that took place, not a person who took it.

We also keep our internal log of what our own staff did, as an accountability
record.

## Changed your mind?

Deletion cannot be undone. If you sign up again with the same number you will
start with a new, empty account — your old trips, ratings and verification will
not come back, and a driver will have to be verified again from the beginning.
