Aap apna UDrive account khud, app ke andr se delete kar sakte hain. Hum se
poochne ki zaroorat hai na koi wajah batane ki.

## App mein

1. **Settings** kholein.
2. **Delete account** par tap karein.
3. Tasdeeq ke liye **DELETE** likhein.

Bas. Yeh foran lagu ho jata hai.

Chalti hui trip ke darmiyan account delete nahi hota — pehle trip mukammal ya
cancel karein, phir dobara koshish karein.

## Agar app na khul rahi ho

WhatsApp par hamein paighaam bhejein: **0335-6823975**, usi number se jis par
account bana hua hai. Hum number ki tasdeeq kar ke account delete kar dein ge.

## Kya mit jata hai

- Aap ka email address mita diya jata hai, aur naam aur phone number ki jagah
  placeholder aa jate hain. **Aap
  ka mobile number foran azad ho jata hai**, to woh dobara register ho sakta hai
  — aap se ya kisi aur se.
- Har device par har khula hua session usi waqt khatam.
- Aap ke trusted contacts poori tarah mita diye jate hain.
- Agar aap UDrive par gaari chalate the: aap ka CNIC number, licence number,
  tareekh-e-paidaish, rehaishi pata, emergency contact aur bank ki tafseel mit
  jati hai, aap ka mehfooz kiya hua payout account mit jata hai, aur **aap ki
  bheji hui CNIC, licence, selfie, registration book, gaari ki tasweerein aur
  wallet top-up ke screenshots hamari storage se mita diye jate hain.**
- Aap ki aakhri maloom jagah mita di jati hai.
- Aap ki gaariyan service se hata di jati hain aur unke registration number azad
  ho jate hain.

## Kya reh jata hai, aur kyun

Jo safar hue un ka record reh jata hai, magar **us par aap ka naam nahi
hota**: booking, fare aur adaigi; rating aur raye; woh tanaza jis mein aap
shaamil the, us ke sath lagai gayi files samet; trip ki chat mein bheje gaye aap
ke paighaam; aur koi bhee emergency alert ya safety report jo aap ne di. Driver
ka trips wala location record foran nahi mitta — woh apne mamool ke mutabiq 30
din mein khatam hota hai.

Hum ise teen wajah se rakhte hain: us safar ke dusre bande ka apne record par
haq hai, hamara hisab barabar hona chahiye, aur kisi safar par tanaza ya tax ka
sawal maheenon baad bhee aa sakta hai. Jo peeche bachta hai woh ek safar hai jo
hua tha — woh shakhs nahi jisne kiya tha.

Hamara apna andaruni record — ke hamare staff ne kya kiya — bhee jawabdehi ke
liye rakha jata hai.

## Irada badal gaya?

Delete wapas nahi hota. Usi number se dobara sign up karne par aap ko ek naya,
khali account milega — purane safar, ratings aur tasdeeq wapas nahi aayengi, aur
driver ko shuru se dobara verify karwana parega.
