## 1 · Sign in and your profile / Sign in aur profile

1. Enter your name and your Pakistani mobile number.
2. Enter the OTP sent to that number.
3. Open **Profile** and add your photograph and an emergency contact.

The emergency contact is not decoration. It is who gets told if you use SOS.

*1. Apna naam aur Pakistani mobile number daalein.*
*2. Usi number par aaya hua OTP daalein.*
*3. **Profile** khol kar apni tasveer aur emergency contact daalein.*

*Emergency contact dikhawa nahi hai. SOS dabane par isi ko ittila jati hai.*

---

## 2 · Home — finding a ride / Home — ride dhoondna

1. Choose **2 Wheel, 3 Wheel or 4 Wheel**.
2. Type your destination, or tap **View all** to browse routes.
3. Open a route card to see the fare, the free seats, the date and the vehicle.

*1. **2 Wheel, 3 Wheel ya 4 Wheel** chunein.*
*2. Manzil likhein, ya **View all** dabayein.*
*3. Kiraya, khaali seats, tareekh aur gaari dekhne ke liye route card kholein.*

---

## 3 · Booking a ride / Ride book karna

1. Set the pickup, the destination, the date, the time and the number of passengers.
2. Where it is offered, choose **per seat** or **the whole vehicle**.
3. Send the request. **Drivers send you their fares — you pick one.** You are not assigned a driver.
4. Compare the offers: the fare, the driver's rating, and the vehicle.
5. Accept one.

*1. Pickup, manzil, tareekh, waqt aur musafiron ki tadaad daalein.*
*2. Jahan mojood ho, **fi seat** ya **poori gaari** chunein.*
*3. Request bhejein. **Driver aap ko apne fare bhejte hain — aap aik chunte hain.** Driver aap par thopa nahi jata.*
*4. Offers ka moqabla karein: kiraya, driver ki rating, aur gaari.*
*5. Aik qubool karein.*

**Careful / Khayal rakhein**
- The cheapest offer is not always the right one. Look at the rating and the vehicle too.
- *Sab se sasta hamesha behtareen nahi hota. Rating aur gaari bhi dekh lein.*

## 4 · During the trip / Safar ke dauran

1. Track the driver on the map until pickup.
2. **Check the registration number against the app before you get in.**
3. Give the driver your **boarding PIN**. Do not give it before you are satisfied the vehicle is the right one — the PIN is what starts the trip.
4. Pay the balance at the end.
5. Rate the driver.

*1. Pickup tak driver ko naqshe par dekhein.*
*2. **Baithne se pehle gaari ka number app se milayein.***
*3. Driver ko apna **boarding PIN** dein. Jab tak itminan na ho ke gaari wahi hai, PIN na dein — PIN hi safar shuru karta hai.*
*4. Aakhir mein baqi raqam adaa karein.*
*5. Driver ko rating dein.*

## 7 · Tour packages / Tour packages

1. Open **Tour packages**.
2. Check the route, the day-by-day plan, what is included, the free seats and the **cancellation rule**.
3. Choose the passengers and how you will pay.
4. **My Trips** shows the booking, the boarding details and the tour's status.

*1. **Tour packages** kholein.*
*2. Route, rozana ka program, kya shamil hai, khaali seats aur **cancel karne ka usool** dekhein.*
*3. Musafir aur adaigi ka tareeqa chunein.*
*4. **My Trips** mein booking, boarding aur tour ki soorat-e-haal nazar aati hai.*

**Careful / Khayal rakhein**
- The cancellation rule is written on the package before you pay. Read it then, not after.
- *Cancel ka usool adaigi se pehle package par likha hota hai. Tab parhein, baad mein nahi.*

## 8 · Renting a car / Gaari kiraye par lena

This is not a ride. You take the car for days, with or without a driver.

1. Choose the dates and whether you want **self-drive** or **with a driver**.
2. Search. Only cars whose owner has switched Rent on, and whose photograph is uploaded, appear.
3. Check the daily rate, the **security deposit**, and the disclaimer.
4. Pay the **advance** through the app.
5. Pay the rest **and the deposit to the owner** when you collect the car.

*Yeh ride nahi hai. Aap gaari dinon ke hisaab se lete hain, driver ke sath ya baghair.*

*1. Tareekhein chunein aur yeh ke **khud chalani** hai ya **driver ke sath**.*
*2. Talash karein. Sirf woh gaariyan dikhti hain jin ke malik ne Rent on kiya ho aur tasveer lagai ho.*
*3. Rozana rate, **security deposit** aur disclaimer dekhein.*
*4. **Advance** app ke zariye dein.*
*5. Baqi raqam **aur deposit malik ko** gaari lete waqt dein.*

**Careful / Khayal rakhein**
- The **security deposit goes to the owner, not to UDrive.** Getting it back is between you and the owner. Count it and get it in writing.
- ***Security deposit malik ke paas jata hai, UDrive ke paas nahi.** Us ki wapsi aap aur malik ke darmiyan hai. Gin lein aur likhwa lein.*
- For self-drive you will be asked for your CNIC and driving licence. Upload clear photographs.
- *Khud chalane ke liye CNIC aur licence maanga jayega. Saaf tasveerein lagayein.*
- Check the car for damage with the owner **before** you drive away, and photograph it.
- *Chalne se **pehle** malik ke samne gaari ka nuqsan dekh lein, aur tasveer le lein.*

## 10 · Payments and refunds / Adaigi aur refund

1. Open the active booking and tap **Pay balance**.
2. Choose the method offered.
3. Cash and bank payments may need an admin to verify them, so they are not instant.
4. Refund status appears in the booking and in your payment history.

*1. Chalti booking khol kar **Pay balance** dabayein.*
*2. Mojood tareeqa chunein.*
*3. Cash aur bank ki adaigi ki admin tasdeeq zaroori ho sakti hai, is liye woh fauri nahi hoti.*
*4. Refund ki soorat-e-haal booking aur payment history mein nazar aati hai.*

## 11 · Safety and SOS / Hifazat aur SOS

- **SOS** is the red button in the bottom bar. Use it only for a real emergency.
- Add **trusted contacts** in Safety first — SOS tells them.
- **Live tracking** works for your own active booking.
- A safety report needs detail: what happened, when, and anything you have as evidence.

*- **SOS** neeche wali bar ka laal button hai. Sirf haqeeqi emergency mein istemal karein.*
*- Pehle Safety mein **trusted contacts** daal lein — SOS unhi ko batata hai.*
*- **Live tracking** aap ki apni chalti booking ke liye kaam karti hai.*
*- Hifazati report mein tafseel chahiye: kya hua, kab hua, aur jo saboot aap ke paas hai.*

## 12 · Notifications / Notifications

**Settings → Notifications.** Seven switches, saved the moment you touch them — no Save button.

| Switch | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Bookings | Driver offers, confirmations, cancellations |
| Packages | Your tour bookings and departures |
| Money | Payments and refunds |
| Complaints | Replies to something you raised |
| Offers | Campaigns and deals |
| Push notifications | On this phone |
| SMS | Only for the important ones |

Turning everything off does **not** stop messages about a trip in progress. Safety messages are always sent.

*Saat switch, chhoote hi save ho jate hain — koi Save button nahi.*

| *Switch* | *Kis ke liye* |
|---|---|
| *Bookings* | *Driver ki offers, confirm, cancel* |
| *Packages* | *Aap ki tour bookings aur rawangi* |
| *Money* | *Adaigi aur refund* |
| *Complaints* | *Aap ki uthai hui baat ka jawab* |
| *Offers* | *Campaign aur deals* |
| *Push notifications* | *Is phone par* |
| *SMS* | *Sirf zaroori baton ke liye* |

*Sab band karne se bhi chalte safar ke paighaam nahi rukte. Hifazat ke paighaam hamesha jate hain.*

## 13 · My business / My business

If you own a hotel, a vehicle or a tour business, this is where you manage it from the customer app. Approval is needed before anything you add is shown to other customers.

*Agar aap ka hotel, gaari ya tour ka kaam hai, to customer app se us ka intezam yahan se hota hai. Jo bhi aap daalein, doosre customers ko dikhne se pehle manzoori zaroori hai.*

---

## 14 · Support and complaints / Madad aur shikayat

- A support ticket is read by a person, not a bot.
- For a complaint about a trip, give the booking reference, what happened, and when.
- Both sides are asked before anything is decided.

*- Support ticket aik insaan parhta hai, bot nahi.*
*- Safar ki shikayat mein booking ka reference, kya hua aur kab hua likhein.*
*- Faisle se pehle dono taraf se poocha jata hai.*

---

## 15 · Settings, language and account / Settings, zuban aur account

- **Language** — English or اردو. The whole app changes.
- **Notifications** — module 12 above.
- **Privacy** and **Terms** open inside the app and work with no internet.
- **Clear cached data** if something looks stale. It does not sign you out.
- **Delete account** removes your account and your personal data permanently. There is no undo.

*- **Language** — English ya اردو. Poori app badal jati hai.*
*- **Notifications** — oopar module 12.*
*- **Privacy** aur **Terms** app ke andar khulte hain, internet ke baghair bhi.*
*- Kuch purana lage to **Clear cached data** dabayein. Is se logout nahi hota.*
*- **Delete account** aap ka account aur zaati maloomat hamesha ke liye khatam kar deta hai. Wapsi ka koi rasta nahi.*
