## 1 · Sign up and verification / Sign up aur tasdeeq

**You cannot go online until an admin approves your documents. There is no way around this and no shortcut.**

1. Open the app, enter your name and your Pakistani mobile number, then the OTP.
2. Switch to **Driver mode** from the menu.
3. Complete the four-step sign-up: CNIC, driving licence, your photograph, and your vehicle.
4. Check every photograph yourself before sending — a blurred CNIC is the single most common reason for a rejection.
5. Send for approval and wait. The result appears on the same screen.

*Jab tak admin aap ke kaghazat manzoor na kare, aap online nahi ja sakte. Is ka koi shortcut nahi.*

1. *App kholein, naam aur Pakistani mobile number daalein, phir OTP.*
2. *Menu se **Driver mode** mein jayein.*
3. *Chaar qadam ka sign-up mukammal karein: CNIC, licence, apni tasveer, aur gaari.*
4. *Bhejne se pehle har tasveer khud dekh lein — dhundli CNIC rejection ki sab se badi wajah hai.*
5. *Approval ke liye bhej kar intezar karein. Nateeja usi screen par aata hai.*

**Careful / Khayal rakhein**
- A rejected document tells you why. Read it before uploading the same photo again.
- *Reject hui dastavez wajah batati hai. Wohi tasveer dobara bhejne se pehle woh parh lein.*

## 2 · Going online / Online jana

The switch is in the **top bar**, not in a menu. While it is off:
- you receive no ride requests,
- your position is not published,
- and no online time is counted towards any reward.

*Switch **oopar wali bar** mein hai, menu mein nahi. Jab tak band hai:*
- *koi ride request nahi aayegi,*
- *aap ki location nahi bheji jati,*
- *aur kisi reward ke liye online waqt nahi ginnta.*

**How online time is counted / Online waqt kaise ginnta hai**

Time is **credited**, not measured from when you switched on. The app sends a heartbeat every minute. Only the gaps between heartbeats that actually arrived close together are counted. A phone that slept in a drawer for an hour and then beat once adds nothing.

*Waqt **credit** hota hai, switch on karne ke waqt se nahi napa jata. App har minute aik heartbeat bhejti hai. Sirf woh waqfe ginte hain jo sach mein qareeb qareeb aaye hon. Jo phone aik ghanta jeb mein band para raha, us ka aik ghanta nahi milega.*

## 4 · Ride requests and sending a fare / Ride request aur fare bhejna

1. A request shows the pickup, the destination, the distance and the time left.
2. Tap it, check the map, and send your fare.
3. The customer sees every driver's offer and picks one. Yours is not the only one.
4. You will see the result on the dashboard.

*1. Request mein pickup, manzil, faasla aur baqi waqt likha hota hai.*
*2. Us par tap karein, naqsha dekhein, aur apna fare bhejein.*
*3. Customer har driver ki offer dekh kar aik chunta hai. Sirf aap ki offer nahi hoti.*
*4. Nateeja dashboard par nazar aa jayega.*

**Careful / Khayal rakhein**
- You need **at least one verified vehicle** before you can send a fare.
- *Fare bhejne se pehle **kam az kam aik verified gaari** zaroori hai.*
- Rejecting requests lowers your acceptance rate, and some rewards are measured on it.
- *Request reject karne se acceptance rate girta hai, aur kuch rewards usi par napte hain.*

## 5 · The trip / Safar

1. Drive to the pickup. The customer can see you on the map.
2. Verify the vehicle with the customer and take the **boarding PIN** from them.
3. Start the trip. Use the navigation screen rather than a separate map app — the trip's status is what everything else reads.
4. Complete the trip. Take the remaining payment.
5. The customer rates you, and you can rate them.

*1. Pickup tak jayein. Customer aap ko naqshe par dekh sakta hai.*
*2. Customer se gaari ki tasdeeq karwayein aur un se **boarding PIN** lein.*
*3. Safar shuru karein. Alag map app ke bajaye navigation screen istemal karein — baqi sab kuch safar ki status hi parhta hai.*
*4. Safar mukammal karein. Baqi raqam lein.*
*5. Customer aap ko rating deta hai, aur aap unhein.*

## 6 · Vehicles / Gaariyan

Each vehicle is registered separately and approved separately.

- Register the vehicle with the correct category — 2-wheel, 3-wheel or 4-wheel.
- Upload the registration book and a clear photograph of the vehicle.
- A vehicle that is not approved cannot be used for anything.

*Har gaari alag register hoti hai aur alag manzoor hoti hai.*

- *Gaari durust category mein register karein — 2-wheel, 3-wheel ya 4-wheel.*
- *Registration book aur gaari ki saaf tasveer upload karein.*
- *Jo gaari manzoor nahi, woh kisi kaam ke liye istemal nahi ho sakti.*

---

## 7 · Vehicle usage — city, tour, rent / Gaari ka istemal

For each vehicle you decide what it is used for. The three switches are independent, with one rule:

| Switch | What it means |
|---|---|
| **City rides** | Ordinary ride-hailing inside the city |
| **Tours** | Tour packages and long routes |
| **Rent** | Someone rents the car for days |

**Rent and City rides cannot both be on.** A car that somebody has rented for three days is not available for a city ride during those three days, and the database refuses the combination outright.

*Har gaari ke liye aap tay karte hain ke woh kis kaam ki hai. Teen switch alag alag hain, magar aik usool ke sath:*

| *Switch* | *Matlab* |
|---|---|
| ***City rides*** | *Shehar ke andar aam rides* |
| ***Tours*** | *Tour package aur lambay route* |
| ***Rent*** | *Koi gaari dinon ke hisaab se kiraye par le* |

***Rent aur City rides ek sath nahi ho sakte.** Jo gaari kisi ne teen din ke liye kiraye par li hai woh un teen dinon mein shehar ki ride ke liye khaali nahi, aur database is jor ko qubool hi nahi karta.*

**To switch Rent on you need / Rent on karne ke liye chahiye**
1. A daily rate, and
2. **a photograph of the vehicle**.

*1. Rozana ka rate, aur*
*2. **gaari ki tasveer**.*

Without both, the switch will not turn on. Nobody rents a car they have not seen.

*Dono ke baghair switch on nahi hoga. Koi bina dekhi gaari kiraye par nahi leta.*

## 8 · Car rental bookings / Kiraye ki bookings

- The customer pays an **advance** through the app. The rest is collected by you on handover.
- The **security deposit stays with you**, not with the platform.
- While a car is rented, it disappears from city dispatch and from tour packages on those dates, and comes back by itself afterwards.
- A booking that clashes with a package departure is refused, and the refusal names the departure.

*- Customer app ke zariye **advance** deta hai. Baqi raqam aap gaari dete waqt lete hain.*
*- **Security deposit aap ke paas rehta hai**, platform ke paas nahi.*
*- Jab tak gaari kiraye par hai, woh un tareekhon mein shehar ki dispatch aur tour packages se gayab rehti hai, aur baad mein khud wapis aa jati hai.*
*- Jo booking kisi package ki rawangi se takrati ho woh reject hoti hai, aur rejection us rawangi ka naam leta hai.*

**Careful / Khayal rakhein**
- Check the CNIC and the licence yourself at handover. The platform shows an admin that documents exist; it does not vouch for them.
- *Gaari dete waqt CNIC aur licence khud dekh lein. Platform admin ko sirf yeh dikhata hai ke kaghazat mojood hain; un ki zimmedari nahi leta.*

## 9 · Routes and tour packages / Route aur tour packages

1. Create a package: the route, the dates, the seats, what is included, and the cancellation rule.
2. It must be approved before customers see it.
3. Customers book whole seats or the whole vehicle.
4. Departures appear under bookings, with the passenger list.

*1. Package banayein: route, tareekhein, seats, kya shamil hai, aur cancel karne ka usool.*
*2. Customers ko dikhne se pehle manzoori zaroori hai.*
*3. Customers seat ya poori gaari book karte hain.*
*4. Rawangiyan bookings mein nazar aati hain, musafiron ki list ke sath.*

## 11 · Wallet / Wallet

- **Balance** is money you can take out.
- **Bonus** is reward money. It behaves differently — read the note on the rewards screen.
- Commission is taken from the fare, not from the wallet afterwards.

*- **Balance** woh paisa hai jo nikala ja sakta hai.*
*- **Bonus** reward ka paisa hai. Us ka tareeqa alag hai — rewards screen par likha note parh lein.*
*- Commission fare se katta hai, baad mein wallet se nahi.*

## 12 · Rewards & missions / Rewards aur missions

This screen is the way in to everything the growth system offers:

- **Welcome bonus** — paid in steps as you get going
- **Open / Finished** missions for today
- **This week** — the weekly target
- **Invite a driver** — your referral code
- **Updates** — what the platform has told your city

*Yeh screen growth system ki har cheez ka darwaza hai:*

- ***Welcome bonus** — qiston mein, jaise jaise aap chalte hain*
- ***Open / Finished** aaj ke missions*
- ***This week** — hafte ka target*
- ***Invite a driver** — aap ka referral code*
- ***Updates** — platform ne aap ke shehar ko kya bataya*

**Why a reward can say "on hold" / Reward "on hold" kyun ho sakta hai**

Three reasons, and the screen tells you which one:
1. The campaign's budget is finished.
2. You have already received the maximum number of that reward.
3. A fraud flag on your account was confirmed by an admin.

*Teen wajah, aur screen batati hai kaunsi:*
*1. Campaign ka budget khatam ho gaya.*
*2. Aap ko woh reward jitni dafa mil sakta tha, mil chuka.*
*3. Aap ke account par aik fraud flag admin ne confirm kar diya.*

## 13 · This week / Yeh hafta

- The week runs **Monday to Sunday, Pakistan time** — the same week the reward is counted against.
- The bar shows your rides against the target, and the line underneath says how many are left.
- **Last week** shows what you earned, how many rides, whether the reward landed, and your **best day**. The best day is the one thing you can act on: it tells you which day to keep clear next week.
- If no weekly target is running in your city, the screen says so plainly rather than drawing a bar against zero.

*- Hafta **peer se itwar tak, Pakistan ke waqt** — usi hafte ke hisaab se reward ginna jata hai.*
*- Bar aap ki rides target ke muqable mein dikhati hai, aur neeche likha hota hai kitni baqi hain.*
*- **Last week** mein kamai, rides, reward mila ya nahi, aur **behtareen din**. Behtareen din hi woh cheez hai jis par aap kuch kar sakte hain: agle hafte woh din khaali rakhein.*
*- Agar shehar mein koi hafte wala target nahi chal raha to screen saaf keh deti hai, zero ki bar nahi banati.*

## 14 · Invite a driver / Driver ko bulayein

**What you get / Aap ko kya milta hai**

| When | Amount |
|---|---|
| The driver you invited gets their documents approved | **PKR 300** |
| They complete their first ride | **PKR 500** |
| They complete 20 rides within 30 days of the first one | **PKR 1,200** |
| **Total per invite** | **PKR 2,000** |

| *Kab* | *Kitna* |
|---|---|
| *Jo driver aap laaye, us ke kaghazat manzoor huay* | ***PKR 300*** |
| *Us ne pehli ride mukammal ki* | ***PKR 500*** |
| *Pehli ride ke 30 din ke andar 20 rides* | ***PKR 1,200*** |
| ***Har invite par kul*** | ***PKR 2,000*** |

Most of the money arrives when they are **actually working**, not when they sign up. That is deliberate.

*Zyada tar paisa tab aata hai jab woh **waqai kaam kar raha ho**, sign-up par nahi. Yeh jaan boojh kar hai.*

**How to invite / Kaise bulayein**
1. Open **Invite a driver**. Tap your code to copy it.
2. **Send on WhatsApp** copies a ready message.
3. They enter your code during sign-up.

*1. **Invite a driver** kholein. Code par tap kar ke copy karein.*
*2. **Send on WhatsApp** se tayyar paighaam copy ho jata hai.*
*3. Woh sign-up ke waqt aap ka code daalta hai.*

**Somebody invited you? / Aap ko kisi ne bulaya?**

Tap **Somebody invited me** and enter their code. But note: a code can only be added **before your account is verified**. After verification the server refuses it, and says so in words.

***Somebody invited me** par tap kar ke un ka code daalein. Magar yaad rahe: code sirf **verification se pehle** daala ja sakta hai. Verification ke baad server mana kar deta hai, aur wajah likh kar batata hai.*

Nothing is taken from you when you enter someone's code. They earn; you lose nothing.

*Kisi ka code daalne se aap ka kuch nahi katta. Unhein milta hai; aap ka kuch nahi jata.*

## 17 · Safety and support / Hifazat aur madad

- SOS is for a real emergency only.
- A complaint needs clear detail and evidence. "The customer was rude" decides nothing; what was said and when does.
- Support tickets are read by a person.

*- SOS sirf haqeeqi emergency ke liye hai.*
*- Shikayat mein saaf tafseel aur saboot chahiye. "Customer badtameez tha" se kuch faisla nahi hota; kya kaha gaya aur kab — us se hota hai.*

## 18 · What gets a driver flagged / Driver par flag kab lagta hai

The system raises a flag in exactly two cases. **Nothing is automatic beyond that — an admin decides.**

1. **MockLocation** — your phone itself tells the server that its position is being faked. This is reported by Android, not guessed by us.
2. **ImpossibleSpeed** — two heartbeats in one online session put you further apart than any road allows: **above 120 km/h**.

*System sirf do soorton mein flag uthata hai. **Is se aagay kuch khud-ba-khud nahi hota — admin faisla karta hai.***

*1. **MockLocation** — aap ka phone khud server ko batata hai ke location jhooti hai. Yeh Android batata hai, hum andaza nahi lagate.*
*2. **ImpossibleSpeed** — aik hi online session ki do heartbeat ke darmiyan itna faasla jo kisi sarak par mumkin nahi: **120 km/h se oopar**.*

**If an admin confirms a flag / Agar admin flag confirm kar de**

- Rewards you have earned but **not yet been paid** are held.
- Money **already in your wallet is not taken back**.
- You are **not blocked**. You keep driving and keep earning fares.
- If the admin clears it later, the held rewards are released.

*- Jo rewards aap ne kama liye magar **abhi mile nahi**, woh ruk jate hain.*
*- Jo paisa **pehle se wallet mein hai woh wapis nahi liya jata**.*
*- Aap **block nahi** hote. Gaari chalate rahein, kiraya kamate rahein.*
*- Admin baad mein clear kar de to ruke hue rewards chal parte hain.*

**The honest advice / Saaf baat**

Do not run location-faking apps on the phone you drive with, even for a game. Android reports them and the flag has your name on it.

*Jis phone se gaari chalate hain us par location badalne wali app na chalayein, chahe kisi game ke liye ho. Android khud batata hai, aur flag par aap ka naam hota hai.*
